# AWS Budgets

## What the Service Is
AWS Budgets gives you the ability to set custom budgets that alert you when your costs or usage exceed (or are forecasted to exceed) your budgeted amount.

## Description of the Service
Budgets tracks Cost, Usage, RI utilization, and Savings Plans coverage. It triggers automated notifications via email or SNS, and can execute AWS Budget Actions (e.g., applying an IAM policy, stopping EC2 instances, or shrinking an Auto Scaling group) when budget thresholds are breached.

## When to Use the Service
- Use AWS Budgets to prevent cost overruns and receive proactive alerts before expenses exceed designated monthly or quarterly limits.
