# Amazon QuickSight

## What the Service Is
Amazon QuickSight is a cloud-native, serverless Business Intelligence (BI) service that enables organizations to deliver interactive dashboards and machine learning insights to users.

## Description of the Service
QuickSight connects to AWS data sources (Redshift, S3, Athena, RDS, Aurora) and third-party data platforms. It uses SPICE (Super-fast, Parallel, In-memory Calculation Engine) to deliver fast analytical response times and offers per-session pay-per-use pricing.

## When to Use the Service
- Use Amazon QuickSight to build, publish, and embed interactive business intelligence dashboards and operational reports.
