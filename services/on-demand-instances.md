# Amazon EC2 On-Demand Instances

## What the Service Is
Amazon EC2 On-Demand Instances let you pay for compute capacity by the second or hour with no long-term commitments or upfront fees.

## When to Use It
- Use On-Demand Instances for short-term, irregular, or newly launched workloads that cannot be interrupted.
- Use during application development and testing phases before usage patterns become predictable.
