# Amazon Kinesis Video Streams

## What the Service Is
Amazon Kinesis Video Streams makes it easy to securely stream live video and audio from connected devices to AWS for analytics, machine learning, and playback.

## Description of the Service
It ingests, encrypts, and indexes live video streams, enabling integration with Amazon Rekognition Video and Amazon SageMaker for real-time computer vision and facial analysis.

## When to Use the Service
- Use Kinesis Video Streams for security camera feeds, smart home devices, industrial robotics, and drone video analytics.
