# Amazon Polly

## What the Service Is
Amazon Polly is a Text-to-Speech (TTS) service that uses advanced deep learning technologies to synthesize natural-sounding human speech from text.

## Description of the Service
Polly supports dozens of lifelike voices across a wide variety of languages. It provides Neural TTS (NTTS) for high vocal quality and supports Speech Synthesis Markup Language (SSML) to control pitch, volume, and speech rate.

## When to Use the Service
- Use Amazon Polly for voice-enabled applications, automated IVR phone announcements (with Amazon Connect), and accessibility features.
